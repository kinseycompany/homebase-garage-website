# Homebase Website Assets

Place images here with these recommended filenames:

## Hero/General
- `hero.jpg` - Hero background image (garage interior or exterior)
- `logo.png` - Homebase logo (if available)

## Feature Photos
- `garage-interior.jpg` - Spacious garage interior shot
- `climate-control.jpg` - Climate control system or comfortable interior
- `electrical.jpg` - Electrical panel or workshop equipment
- `security.jpg` - Gate, cameras, or security features

## Use Case Photos
- `cars.jpg` - Car collection storage
- `rv-boat.jpg` - RV or boat in garage
- `workshop.jpg` - Workshop/maker space setup
- `business.jpg` - Business storage use
- `mancave.jpg` - Man cave setup (lounge, gym, etc.)

## Amenities
- `clubhouse.jpg` - Clubhouse interior or exterior
- `community.jpg` - Community gathering or outdoor area

## Recommended Specs
- Format: JPG or WebP
- Size: 1920x1080 minimum for hero, 800x600 for cards
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 4:3 for consistency
